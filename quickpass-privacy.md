# QuickPass Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 9, 2026**

QuickPass is designed to generate passwords locally on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how QuickPass handles information when you use the current version of the application.

## Information We Collect

The current version of QuickPass does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the app. QuickPass does not collect personal information through the app.

## Password Generation

QuickPass generates passwords locally on your device using cryptographically secure randomness. Generated passwords are not sent to QuickPass servers, cloud services, or external APIs.

The current version of QuickPass does not transmit generated passwords over the internet.

## Password Storage

The current version of QuickPass does not permanently store generated passwords.

QuickPass may temporarily keep recently generated passwords in memory while the app is running so they can appear in the password history. This temporary history is not saved to persistent storage and is cleared when the app is closed or restarted.

## Clipboard

QuickPass copies a generated password to your device's clipboard only when you choose the Copy action.

After a password is copied, the clipboard is managed by your device's operating system. Other applications may be able to access clipboard contents depending on your device, operating system, permissions, and settings.

The current version of QuickPass does not automatically clear copied passwords from the clipboard.

## Network and Third-Party Services

The current version of QuickPass does not use a backend service to generate, store, or process passwords and does not transmit generated passwords to external services.

QuickPass does not currently include advertising, analytics, user tracking, or cloud synchronization.

## Permissions and Device Features

QuickPass uses device features only as needed to provide its functionality. This includes clipboard access when you choose to copy a password and haptic feedback where supported.

QuickPass does not request access to sensitive device information such as your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location as part of its current functionality.

## Data Sharing

The current version of QuickPass does not sell, rent, or share your personal information or generated passwords with third parties.

Because generated passwords are created locally and are not transmitted by QuickPass, QuickPass does not send generated passwords to third parties for advertising, analytics, or other purposes.

## Children's Privacy

QuickPass is not designed to collect personal information from users, including children. The current version does not require an account or request personal information to use the app.

If QuickPass introduces features in the future that collect personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate before those features are made available.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as QuickPass evolves or if its features, legal requirements, or data practices change.

If a future version introduces features such as user accounts, cloud synchronization, persistent password storage, advertising, analytics, or other functionality that changes how information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

The effective date at the top of this policy will indicate when the current version of the policy became effective.
